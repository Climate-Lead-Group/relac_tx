# Methodology

The study applies a regional modelling framework using OSeMOSYS to evaluate long-term electricity system expansion strategies across the Latin American and Caribbean countries represented in the model. A harmonised dataset was developed to ensure comparability and consistency among national models. Calibration relied on official national planning documents, transmission expansion plans and investment roadmaps, supported by data from national system operators and international databases.

The scenario families described in {doc}`scenarios` explore the system's behaviour under different levels of climate ambition, renewable energy penetration, repowering strategies and cost assumptions. The model estimates generation and transmission expansion needs, focusing on cross-border interconnections, system flexibility and infrastructure bottlenecks. These country-level insights are synthesised into regional strategies that support RELAC's 2030 target of 80% renewable electricity generation.

The modelling tool itself, its data pipeline and its configuration are documented in the *Getting started*, *Pipeline* and *Reference* sections of this site.
